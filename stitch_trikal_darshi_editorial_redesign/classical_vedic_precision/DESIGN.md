## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intellectual rigor, classical authority, and celestial geometry of Vedic astrology (Jyotish Shastra), balanced by an editorial warmth and clarity that eschews occult tropes or mystical clutter. The interface functions as a scholar’s illuminated manuscript rendered with modern architectural precision: grounded in parchment-like tactile warmth, structured by hairline coordinate grids, and punctuated by fine astral gold accents.

### Visual Character & Emotional Tone
- **Authoritative & Scholarly:** Presents complex planetary calculations, dasha timelines, and divisional charts (Kundali) with mathematical credibility rather than vague mysticism.
- **Warm Tactility:** The foundation is defined by warm parchment paper tones rather than clinical device whites, evoking hand-milled rag paper, vellum charts, and enduring observatory instruments.
- **Geometric Rigor:** Uses delicate, linear 1px vectors for North/South Indian chart formats, yantras, nakshatra diagrams, and planetary aspect lines. 
- **Subtle Astral Atmosphere:** Incorporates an understated light-mode starfield composed of sparse, ultra-fine gold coordinate nodes and constellations overlaid across the warm ivory background. No neon flares, nebulas, or opaque saturated wallpapers.
- **Single-State Permanence:** Exclusively light mode. The visual weight relies on the dynamic between deep ink and warm paper surfaces.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic relationship of traditional indigo ink and mineral gold leaf against warm ivory paper. Color is applied with functional discipline to preserve cognitive legibility across dense astrological data tables and multi-layered charts.

### Palette Architecture
- **Paper / Base Surface (`#FBF6EA`):** A warm, light ivory unbleached canvas that eliminates display glare and forms the background foundation.
- **Raised Surface (`#FFFDF6`):** A pristine, warm tinted card and modal plane providing clear surface separation above `#FBF6EA`.
- **Ink / Primary Text (`#16223F`):** Deep, saturated midnight ink delivering crisp legibility (>13:1 contrast ratio against Paper and Raised surfaces).
- **Ink-Soft / Secondary Text (`#4A567A`):** Muted slate navy for metadata, house coordinates, degree readings, and secondary labels (>4.5:1 contrast against base paper).
- **Blue / Primary Action (`#1F3A6B`):** Deep oceanic indigo reserved for primary interaction targets, selected states, and structural chart framing.
- **Blue-Deep / Active & Hover (`#12244A`):** High-density dark navy applied to interactive hover/pressed states, deep border delineations, and high-contrast text placed over gold fills.
- **Gold / Celestial Accent (`#D9A63C`):** Radiant metallic ochre for active navigation markers, benefic indicators, planetary dignity highlights, and focal coordinate pins.
- **Gold-Soft / Accent Tints (`#F0DFAF`):** Gentle champagne gold wash for selected chart zones, badge backings, quote containers, and highlighted divisional houses.
- **Error (`#BA1A1A`):** Traditional vermilion red reserved for malefic afflictions, input validation errors, and critical planetary conflicts.

### Contrast & Functional Guidelines
- Deep Ink (`#16223F`) is the required fill for all primary typography on `#FBF6EA` and `#FFFDF6`.
- When Gold (`#D9A63C`) is used as a button or badge surface, paired text and glyphs must be Blue-Deep (`#12244A`) or Ink (`#16223F`) to satisfy WCAG AA legibility. White text on Gold is prohibited.
- Hairline borders use `#1F3A6B` rendered at 12% to 20% opacity (`rgba(31, 58, 107, 0.15)`), producing structural definition that mimics fine architectural pen strokes.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial cadence, matching the literary pedigree of classical commentaries with the tabular precision demanded by astronomical calculations.

### Type Roles & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in `Fraunces` with font-weight calibrated between `480` and `600`. Fraunces leverages soft optical sizing curves to echo chisel-cut serifs and manuscript calligraphy. Fallbacks to `Noto Serif Devanagari` and `Noto Serif Bengali` ensure cultural cohesion when rendering Sanskrit terminology (such as *Graha*, *Rashi*, and *Nakshatra*).
- **Body & Tabular Reading:** Set in `Inter` with fallbacks to `Noto Sans Devanagari` and `Noto Sans Bengali`. Clean neutral architecture maximizes legibility within dasha timelines, planetary aspect matrices, and long-form horoscopic synthesis.
- **Astrological Coordinates & Glyphs:** Degree notations, sign abbreviations, and coordinate labels use `label-md` and `label-sm` with active tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to maintain geometric column alignment across planetary tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt micro-subdivision for dense astrological tables and diagrammatic layouts. The composition reflects architectural proportions, using intentional margins to evoke an open editorial broadsheet.

### Structural Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid constrained to a maximum width of `80rem` (1280px). Standard gutters are `2rem` (`space-xl`) with outer canvas margins of `2rem` to `3rem`. This layout accommodates side-by-side divisional charts alongside interpretative commentary.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` (`gutter-md`) gutters and `1.5rem` screen padding. Charts transition into a balanced 2-column stacking model.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (`gutter-sm`) gutters and `1rem` outer horizontal margins. Astrological charts scale down while maintaining their 1:1 aspect ratio, with secondary metadata shifting directly beneath the visual frame.

### Chart Grid Constraints
Planetary charts (both North Indian diamond schemas and South Indian square cartographies) are locked to strict square ratio containers (`aspect-ratio: 1 / 1`). All internal house dividers must snap to crisp hairline vectors (0.5px to 1px) aligned to the internal coordinate system.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via surface layering and hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows. The design system uses subtle, paper-tinted ambient shifts to preserve an organic, print-like aesthetic.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF6EA`):** The primary paper ground plane. Houses structural canvas elements, page titles, and background astronomical coordinate maps.
- **Tier 1 - Raised Panels (`#FFFDF6`):** The standard elevation for cards, chart containers, and analytical modules. Outlined with a 1px border of `#1F3A6B` at 10% opacity (`rgba(31, 58, 107, 0.10)`).
- **Tier 2 - Floating Drawers & Tooltips (`#FFFDF6`):** Used for planetary inspect popovers and contextual flyouts. Uses a crisp 1px border of `#D9A63C` at 35% opacity alongside an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 34, 63, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 34, 63, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 - Critical Modals (`#FFFDF6`):** Paired with a back-layer dimming tint of `#16223F` at 35% opacity. Outlined with a solid 1px border of `#1F3A6B` at 18% opacity, accompanied by `box-shadow: 0 20px 36px -8px rgba(22, 34, 63, 0.14)`.

### Decorative Linear Geometry
To reflect traditional astronomical apparatus:
- Card corners optionally feature 4px geometric corner tick marks using `#D9A63C` at 40% opacity.
- Dividing rules use a dual-tone 1px stroke: a solid linear hair rule of `#1F3A6B` at 12% opacity, occasionally punctuated at the center by a 4px diamond node (`✦`) in `#D9A63C`.
- The background starfield effect utilizes subtle scalar vector dots (`#D9A63C` at 25% to 35% opacity, 1px to 2px size) positioned at calculated coordinate intervals.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects classic bookmaking and astronomical drafts: soft, restrained, and structured. Overly bulbous pills or hyper-rounded components are avoided in favor of crisp, precise geometry.

### Radius Distribution
- **Sharp Geometry (`0px`):** Astrological divisional charts, yantra borders, timeline dasha ribbons, and planetary grid tables.
- **Base Components (`roundedness: 1` / `0.25rem` / `4px`):** Standard interactive elements including input fields, action buttons, tooltips, tags, and table cells.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Raised content surfaces, planetary overview panels, Kundali display cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Large Panels (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / `12px`):** Root-level surface containers and overarching application shells.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid Blue (`#1F3A6B`) fill with high-contrast text in Raised Surface White (`#FFFDF6`), 4px border-radius, `0.5rem 1.25rem` padding. In hover state, transition smoothly to Blue-Deep (`#12244A`). Focus state adds a 2px offset ring rendered in Gold (`#D9A63C`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with 1px border in Blue (`#1F3A6B` at 25% opacity), text in Blue (`#1F3A6B`). In hover state, background shifts to Blue (`#1F3A6B` at 6% opacity) with the border firming to 50% opacity.
- **Tertiary Accent (Celestial Action):** Solid Gold (`#D9A63C`) fill with Blue-Deep (`#12244A`) typography. Hover state deepens to `#C4922B`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Input:** Background of `#FFFDF6` enclosed in a 1px border of `#1F3A6B` (at 20% opacity). Typography uses Ink (`#16223F`) with placeholder values in Ink-Soft (`#4A567A` at 65% opacity). Height is standardized to 40px (desktop) / 44px (touch).
- **Active / Focused:** Border shifts to a crisp 1px Gold (`#D9A63C`) with an ambient 2px outer aura (`rgba(217, 166, 60, 0.20)`).
- **Error State:** Border shifts to Error (`#BA1A1A`) with supporting helper text rendered in `#BA1A1A`.

### Planetary Chips & Zodiac Badges
- **Status & Dignity Badges (Exalted, Debilitated, Retrograde):** Background of Gold-Soft (`#F0DFAF`) carrying Ink (`#16223F`) or Blue-Deep (`#12244A`) typography; 4px radius; `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding.
- **Afflicted / Combust Chips:** Soft vermilion wash (`rgba(186, 26, 26, 0.08)`) with `#BA1A1A` 1px border and text.
- **Interactive Planetary Filters:** Background in `#FFFDF6`, 1px border in `#1F3A6B` at 15% opacity. When toggled active: background switches to `#1F3A6B` with white text and a gold indicator dot.

### Astrological Chart Cards (Kundali Viewers)
- **Container Structure:** Raised Surface (`#FFFDF6`) framed with a 1px border of `#1F3A6B` at 12% opacity.
- **Chart Lines:** Internal house delineations are rendered as razor-sharp 1px lines (`rgba(31, 58, 107, 0.25)`).
- **Ascendant / Kendra Highlight:** Cardinal houses (1, 4, 7, 10) feature an optional subtle gradient wash of Gold-Soft (`rgba(240, 223, 175, 0.25)`).
- **Coordinate Tags:** Sign degrees rendered in Ink-Soft (`#4A567A`) using `label-sm` with tabular numerals.

### Lists & Timeline Tables (Dasha Cycles)
- **Structure:** Clean tabular alignment with borderless row partitions; separated only by a 1px horizontal rule (`rgba(31, 58, 107, 0.08)`).
- **Active Time Period:** Marked by a solid 3px vertical accent bar in Gold (`#D9A63C`) placed on the left margin, with the row receiving a subtle warm fill of Gold-Soft (`rgba(240, 223, 175, 0.35)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Unselected:** 1px border of `#1F3A6B` at 30% opacity on a `#FFFDF6` base.
- **Selected:** Solid fill of `#1F3A6B` with a crisp white checkmark or inner dot. Focused states project a 2px Gold (`#D9A63C`) focus ring.
